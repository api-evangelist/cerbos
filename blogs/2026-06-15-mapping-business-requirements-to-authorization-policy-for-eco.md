This article demonstrates how to translate real-world e-commerce business requirements into authorization policies using Cerbos. It covers three scenarios — customer data access, vendor product management, and order lifecycle control — showing why basic role-based access control falls short and how attribute-based policy controls provide the granularity modern marketplaces require.
